# TEDxLe Quy Don HS Binh Dinh — Brand Summary

**Event:** TEDxLe Quy Don HS Binh Dinh 2026  
**Theme:** Mã (Chess Knight / Code)  
**Date:** June 28, 2026 · Gia Lai, Vietnam  
**Audience:** Students, local community, Gia Lai province  
**Ticket:** 139,000 VND · 150+ attendees · 8 speakers

## One-line identity
A student-led TEDx event that gives young voices in Gia Lai a stage — bold, dark, editorial.

> **2026-10-17 update:** the organisation is now **TEDxLe Quy Don Gia Lai Youth** with the identity **"Nét"** (key visual `images/Theme.jpg`: cream paper, pink folding fan with cherry blossoms, layered pastel cloud corners, blackletter "Nét" in plum). The site is a team + season-recap page — no ticketing. TED red only on the "TEDx" wordmark. Current tokens live in `docs/design-system.md`; the notes below describe season "Mã".

## Feeling in 3 seconds
Dark. Confident. Purposeful. The design feels like a world-class event happened here.

## Primary color
Red `#eb0028` — TED's signature, used sparingly for CTAs and accents only.

## Canvas
Near-black `#141414` — not pure black. Warm, not cold.

## Typography
- **Display:** Fraunces (serif, high-contrast, editorial weight)
- **Body:** Be Vietnam Pro (Vietnamese-optimized sans-serif)
- **Accent:** Playfair Display italic (quotes, taglines only)
- **Hero only:** UTM Euphoria (local font, "Mã" title only — do not use elsewhere)

## Voice
Vietnamese. Direct. Second-person. Youth-facing but not juvenile.
"Ý tưởng đáng được lan tỏa" — not "Unleash your potential."
