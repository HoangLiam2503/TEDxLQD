# Logo Usage

## Official name
**TEDxLe Quy Don HS Binh Dinh**

Note: "Le Quy Don" is the transliteration of "Lê Quý Đôn" (no diacritics in the TEDx name per TED licensing convention).

## TEDx brand rules (non-negotiable)
- "TED" must always appear in TED red (`#eb0028`) or white — never gray, never black
- The lowercase "x" must immediately follow "TED" with no space
- The event name follows "TEDx" — "TEDxLe Quy Don HS Binh Dinh"
- Do not alter proportions or recolor the TED portion

## Clearspace
Minimum clearspace around the lockup: equal to the height of the "x" character on all sides.

## Backgrounds
- Preferred: dark (`#141414` or `#1c1d1d`)
- Acceptable: white or very light gray (use the dark variant of the logo)
- Never: on a busy photographic background without a solid color backing

## Do not
- Stretch or distort the lockup
- Add drop shadows to the wordmark
- Use on red backgrounds
- Use unauthorized colors for "TED"

## Files to produce
When creating logo assets, produce:
- `wordmark.svg` — full name horizontal
- `mark.svg` — "TEDx" only (short form)
- `favicon.svg` — minimal mark for browser tab
- WebP/PNG renders at 1×, 2× for each
