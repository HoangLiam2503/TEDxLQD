# Brand

## Identity
**TEDxLe Quy Don HS Binh Dinh** — a student-organized TEDx event at Lê Quý Đôn Specialized High School, Gia Lai province, Vietnam. Independently licensed by TED.

**2026 Theme: Mã**
"Mã" is the Vietnamese word for the Chess Knight — a piece that moves in unconventional paths. It also resonates with "Code." The theme celebrates young people who think differently, move unexpectedly, and create change through strategic, bold action.

## Event facts
- **Date:** June 28, 2026
- **Location:** Gia Lai, Vietnam
- **Speakers:** 8
- **Audience:** 150+ attendees
- **Ticket price:** 139,000 VND
- **Contact:** tedxlequydonbinhdinh@gmail.com

## Visual personality
Dark. Editorial. Confident. The design communicates that a world-class event is happening in Gia Lai — not a school project, a real TEDx. The near-black canvas and editorial type signal ambition; the TED red grounds it in the global brand.

## Logo / Wordmark
The brand uses the standard TEDx lockup: "TEDx" prefix + "Le Quy Don HS Binh Dinh" in a secondary weight. The "x" is lowercase per TED guidelines. The official red (#eb0028) is mandatory for "TED" — do not recolor.

## What this brand is not
- Not a generic school event (no clip art, no rainbow colors)
- Not a startup (no purple gradients, no glassmorphism)
- Not a corporate conference (no stock "diverse team" photos)
