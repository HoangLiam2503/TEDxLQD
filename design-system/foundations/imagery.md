# Imagery

## Direction
Real photography of actual speakers and attendees. No stock photos. No AI-generated humans.

## Speaker portraits
- Aspect ratio: 3:4 (portrait)
- Display size: 600px wide max
- Format: WebP (converted from source JPG/PNG)
- Quality: 82–90 (sharp enough for faces at card size)
- Object fit: cover, centered; adjust `object-position` per portrait
- Style on desktop: grayscale(30%) at rest, full color on hover

## Event photography guidelines
- Shoot in available light — the dark aesthetic matches the web canvas
- Avoid flash-white backgrounds
- Tight framing preferred (face + shoulders for speaker portraits)
- No posed "conference" stock aesthetics

## What to avoid
- Stock "diverse team around a laptop"
- AI-generated portraits
- Generic "innovation" visuals (glowing orbs, neural nets, wireframe globes)
- Overly bright or white-background images on the dark canvas
